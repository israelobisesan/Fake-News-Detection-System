==============================================================================
WHY TWO EXPERIMENTS?
==============================================================================
Every genuine article in ISOT is Reuters wire copy and carries a
'(Reuters)' marker; almost no fake article does. A model can therefore score
very highly by recognising the publisher instead of the writing style, which
would not generalise to real-world news. The de-leaked run removes those
markers so the reported score reflects language rather than provenance.

  Model                        Naive F1  De-leaked F1  Difference
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Multinomial Naive Bayes        0.9580        0.9476     -0.0103
  Calibrated Linear SVM          0.9946        0.9883     -0.0064
  Random Forest                  0.9912        0.9692     -0.0220

These are validation-set figures. The deployed model is the de-leaked run.

==============================================================================
INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================
A high score on ISOT does NOT mean the system is accurate on news from other
countries, other publishers or other eras. ISOT's fake half comes from a small
group of unreliable outlets and its genuine half is homogeneous Reuters copy, so
the benchmark is far easier than real-world misinformation detection. Treat the
figures above as a measurement on THIS dataset, not as a general capability.
