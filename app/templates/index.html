How the classification works

  1. Your headline and article text are combined into a single input.
  2. The text is cleaned: lowercased, punctuation and numbers removed, stop words dropped and words reduced to their root form.
  3. Publisher markers such as wire-service datelines and URLs are removed, so the model judges the writing rather than the source.
  4. TF-IDF converts the cleaned text into numeric features using the vocabulary learned during training.
  5. The trained classifier predicts a label and a probability, which becomes the confidence score.
