How the classification works

  1. Your headline and article text are combined into a single input.
  2. If you supplied a link instead of pasting, the page is fetched and the headline and body are read from it. Private and internal addresses are refused, and the text must be substantial enough to be a real article.
  3. The text is cleaned: lowercased, punctuation and numbers removed, stop words dropped and words reduced to their root form.
  4. Publisher markers such as wire-service datelines and URLs are removed, so the model judges the writing rather than the source.
  5. TF-IDF converts the cleaned text into numeric features using the vocabulary learned during training.
  6. The trained classifier predicts a label and a probability, which becomes the confidence score.
